# Promo banner brief: Invo Tech Inc. on the Unity Asset Store

## Where it shows

- The banner sits at the top of the Invo Tech Inc. publisher page on the Unity Asset Store.
- Unity says it "will generally be what visitors see first".
- Unity also shows it on our package pages during Asset Store sales.
- Our demo video fills the slot until the banner is uploaded.
- Upload path: Publisher Portal > Profile > Promo banner > Image upload.

## Size and format

- **Master file:** 2400 × 800 px PNG (3:1). Also export a 1950 × 1300 px version (3:2), so whichever shape the page crops to is covered.
- **Unity sets no size,** so we'll check how it crops with "View on Asset Store" after uploading.
- **Safe area:** keep the logo, headline and anything important inside the centre 1600 × 600 px. Edges may be cropped on narrow screens.
- **File size:** under 2 MB. No text smaller than 28 px at 2400 px wide.

## Message

- **Headline:** In-game currency that moves between games
- **Sub-line (optional):** Top up, spend, send and transfer across every game on Invo Network. Free Unity SDK.
- **Hide anything else:** URLs, prices, "Free" badges and Unity's logo. Unity forbids its logo, and the store already shows the price.

## Content

- **Logo:** the INVO "iv" mark, white on its purple gradient tile, plus the "Invo Network" wordmark.
- **One clear visual idea:** a single balance flowing between several games, ending in a phone approving it.
  - It must not show a real company's game art, icons or characters. Use abstract game tiles or our own placeholder art.
- **Optional:** a real capture of the SDK window, for example the Send > "Choose a game" screen or the QR approval screen.
  - Use the full-resolution captures in `Docs/screenshots/6000.0/`.
  - Use them unedited, so the store doesn't show UI that doesn't exist.

## Brand

- **Background:** near-black navy `#0B0E18` with soft purple glows.
- **Accent gradient:** `#6456FF` to `#8B3DFF` (the logo tile).
- **Text:**
  - primary `#F0F2F8`
  - secondary `#A0A8BE`
  - action blue in the SDK UI `#3A6FE0`
- **Font:** Inter (Bold for the headline, Medium for the rest). It ships with the SDK under the SIL Open Font License.

## Don'ts

- No third-party game art, logos or trademarks. That includes Unity's logo, platform-holder logos (PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo, Apple, Google, Steam) and other studios' games.
- No claims we can't back up: user numbers, "#1", or partner names without written permission.
- No AI-generated imagery unless we disclose it on the package's Description tab.
